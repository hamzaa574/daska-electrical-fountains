About Daska Group

Founded in 1965 by Mian Muhammad Akram, Daska Group of Industries has maintained an "A" class market reputation in water pumps, tubewells, and commercial fountains for decades. Continued by Mian Muhammad Mudassar, we maintain high standards of precision engineering.

We manufacture and import international standard products from Germany, Italy, France, Australia, Canada, Mexico, Sweden, and China, ensuring superior reliability for every client nationwide.

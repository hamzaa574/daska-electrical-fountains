About Us

Established in 1965

Founded by Mir Muhammad Akram, Daska Group has maintained an "A-Class" reputation for honesty, dynamic effort, and superlative manufacturing standards. Managed by Mir Muhammad Mudassar, we deal in high-quality pumps, boring equipment, and water systems.

Global Imports: Germany, Italy, France, Australia, Canada, Mexico, Sweden, and China.

Quality Assurance: ISO 9001 quality control standards with advanced test beds and custom tooling for precision manufacturing.

Our Products

Vertical Turbine Pumps

Long cylindrical design, easy maintenance, and compact footprint. Ideal for industrial water treatment, power plants, irrigation, and mine dewatering.

Submersible & Mud Pumps

Heavy-duty wastewater/solids handling pumps (2" to 6" sizes) with a 1-year warranty. Includes screw and multilevel submersible pumps.

Multi-Stage & Centrifugal Pumps

High-head CR/CM series centrifugal pumps, horizontal multistage pumps, and BMS high-pressure booster modules.

Reverse Osmosis (R.O.) Plants

Custom-designed R.O. plants for sea water, well water, factory wastewater, and municipal water purification. Serving mineral water companies across Pakistan.

Drip Irrigation Systems

Micro-irrigation networks (valves, pipes, emitters) to save water and deliver nutrients directly to plant roots.

Rain Guns & Impact Sprinklers

Corrosion-resistant plastic impact sprinklers and high-volume rain guns for efficient field coverage.

Dairy Farm Showering Systems

Climate & temperature control sprinkler systems for dairy cows to prevent heat stress and increase productivity.

Fountains & Water Features

Decorative fountains, artificial waterfalls, custom nozzles, and waterproof underwater LED lighting systems.

Swimming Pools & Accessories

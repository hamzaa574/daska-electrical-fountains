About Our Legacy

Excellence and precision in water management systems for decades

Established in 1965

Founded by Mir Muhammad Akram, Daska Group has maintained an "A-Class" reputation for honesty, dynamic effort, and superlative manufacturing standards. Managed by Mir Muhammad Mudassar, we deal in high-quality pumps, boring equipment, and water systems.


Global Imports: Germany, Italy, France, Australia, Canada, Mexico, Sweden, and China.

Quality Assurance: ISO 9001 quality control standards with advanced test beds and custom tooling for precision manufacturing.
